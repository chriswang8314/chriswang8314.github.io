Scene stills

Paints every scene in scenes/index.js once at its day and night placeholder hours (from the scene's placeholder), at 1600 × 1215 (one 1600 × 900 screen plus its dissolve) and at 960 wide, plus a gallery thumbnail. Save each file into scenes/stills/ under the name shown. Serve the site locally and open this page from it (/tools/stills.html).
